## The opportunity

Political polling is a growing ~$9B market, but it has one **unsolved problem** and
one **structural gap**:

- **Unsolved problem:** across 2016, 2020, and 2024, the polls missed in the same
  direction because of *differential partisan nonresponse* — Republican and
  low-trust voters answer at lower rates even within demographic cells, and
  weighting alone can't fix it.
- **Structural gap:** the most advanced polling technology in the country —
  individual-level modeling at scale (David Shor / Blue Rose), real-time adaptive
  sampling (Change Research), productized message-testing, and compounding data
  cooperatives (Catalist) — lives almost entirely on the **Democratic** side.
  Catalist is closed to Republicans; the GOP incumbents (Cygnal, WPA, Fabrizio,
  Trafalgar) each own *one* axis and none combines them with transparency.

**The firm:** a technology-heavy, low-headcount **Republican** polling and
voter-analytics firm that ports the Democratic technical frontier onto the right,
attacks nonresponse at the contact layer, and wins credibility through radical
transparency — engineered to become one of the most accurate in the country.

## Why it wins — the moat

1. **Individual-level modeling at scale** (MRP + ML + Bayesian over a continuous
   large-sample stream) — the Blue Rose paradigm, which the GOP side lacks.
2. **A proprietary data flywheel** — every interview ever fielded, keyed to voter
   IDs, sharpening the next model. This is the Republican answer to Catalist, and
   it compounds cycle over cycle into a durable advantage.
3. **A message-testing product** — recurring revenue and a defensible asset with
   *no* conservative equivalent today (open white space).
4. **Radical transparency + no herding** — the opposite of every incumbent's
   instinct, and exactly what independent pollster ratings reward.

## Why it's a good business

- **Tech-heavy, low-headcount:** a 2–4 person core (founder + senior quant + data
  engineer + contractors) runs a credible multi-mode operation because owning the
  voter-file frame + text-to-web + MRP + an automated pipeline replaces a call
  center.
- **Diversified, recurring revenue:** benchmark & tracking polls, district-level
  MRP estimates, turnout/persuasion modeling, subscription tracking dashboards, and
  the message-testing library — smoothing the election-cycle boom-bust with
  advocacy and corporate/public-affairs work.
- **Attractive unit economics:** ~$5k–$20k per credible statewide multi-mode poll;
  ~$30k–$80k/yr fixed to operate lean.

## What ~30 days delivers

A **launch-ready operation**: legal entity, data licenses, field + modeling stack,
an automated field→weight→MRP→crosstab→dashboard pipeline, the data flywheel
capturing every interview from Day 1, and a **first publicly released,
fully-disclosed benchmark poll**. (Week-by-week plan:
[07-30-day-launch-plan.md](./07-30-day-launch-plan.md).)

> **Honest framing:** 30 days builds the *machine*, not a finished reputation.
> "Most accurate in the country" is earned over 2–3 cycles of published, verifiable
> results — which is exactly what this operation is engineered to produce.

## The team we need

- **Founder** — 30 years in politics, government affairs, and grassroots; owns
  strategy, relationships, and electoral judgment.
- **Senior quant / methodologist (the "Shor seat")** — the single most important
  hire; owns MRP, ML, weighting, and accuracy. ([Hiring spec.](./10-hiring-senior-quant.md))
- **Data engineer / ops** — pipeline, warehouse, sampling engine, flywheel,
  dashboards (can start fractional).

## What we hold firm on

- **AI is upstream-only** — drafting, coding, automation; **never** for producing
  published estimates, and never "synthetic respondents."
- **We never herd**, and **the model never rescues a biased sample** — contact
  quality comes first.
- **We out-transparent everyone** — AAPOR Transparency Initiative from day one.
